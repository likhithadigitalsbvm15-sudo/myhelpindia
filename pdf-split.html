✂️ PDF Splitter

Extract selected pages from a PDF

📄 Select PDF

Choose your PDF file

📑 Select Pages

Examples:
• 1 → Page 1 only
• 1,3,5 → Pages 1, 3 and 5
• 2-5 → Pages 2, 3, 4 and 5
• 1,4-6,9 → Pages 1, 4, 5, 6 and 9
How to use:
1. Select a PDF file.
2. Enter the pages you want.
3. Click Split PDF.
4. The selected pages will be downloaded as a new PDF.

ℹ️ About PDF Splitter

This tool extracts selected pages from a PDF and creates a new PDF file. Your PDF is processed in your browser and is not uploaded to a server.
