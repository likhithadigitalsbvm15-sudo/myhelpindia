📄 Document Scanner

Scan your document and save it as a clean PDF

📷 Select Document Photo

How to use:
1. Select or take a photo of your document.
2. Choose scan quality.
3. Click Scan Document.
4. Click Download PDF to save it.

ℹ️ About Document Scanner

This tool converts a document photo into a clean PDF. Processing happens in your browser. Your photo is not uploaded to a server.
