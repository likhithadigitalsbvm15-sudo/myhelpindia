💧 PDF Watermark

Add your own text watermark to a PDF

📄 Select PDF

Choose your PDF file

How to use:
1. Select a PDF file.
2. Enter your watermark text.
3. Choose size, position and opacity.
4. Click Add Watermark.
5. Your watermarked PDF will be downloaded.

ℹ️ About PDF Watermark

Add a custom text watermark to every page of your PDF. Your PDF is processed in your browser and is not uploaded to a server.
