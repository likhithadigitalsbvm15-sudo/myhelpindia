✍️ PDF Signature

Sign your PDF documents easily and professionally

1. Select PDF

🔒 Your PDF is processed inside your browser. It is not uploaded to a server.

2. Create Your Signature

✍️ Use your mouse on computer or finger on mobile to draw your signature.

3. Signature Settings

⚠️ This tool creates a visible image-based signature. It is not a cryptographic/digital certificate signature.

How It Works

1️⃣ Select your PDF file.

2️⃣ Draw your signature above.

3️⃣ Select the page and signature position.

4️⃣ Choose the signature size and opacity.

5️⃣ Click Sign PDF & Download.

💡 The signed PDF will be downloaded automatically.
