Rotate PDF pages and save a new PDF

📄 Select PDF

Choose your PDF file

How to use:
1. Select your PDF file.
2. Choose the rotation angle.
3. Click Rotate PDF.
4. The rotated PDF will be downloaded.

ℹ️ About PDF Rotate

This tool rotates all pages of your PDF and creates a new PDF file. Your PDF is processed in your browser and is not uploaded to a server.
